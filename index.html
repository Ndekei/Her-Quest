The Reality

She never stops
searching.

A woman is known to always be on a search; a quest for the best recipe, dress, home, shopping deal, beauty therapy, and not to forget, the perfect man! Sadly, it seems like she never gets to the treasure.

While on this quest, the woman oscillates between four forms of identity, each with different characteristics. It takes her through a roller coaster of spiritual and emotional experiences that leave her feeling lost and unsure of her identity.

Our Story

How Her Quest
began.

The woman is the center and subject of many conversations, debates and agendas across nations, tribes and religions all around the world. So much has been said and expected of her, without her consultation or consent. Many closed-door meetings have been held, while the woman waits outside to know her fate.

There is a way she is supposed to talk, dress, think and behave; there is a time she is supposed to get married or even get children. The question is, who took the time to explain to her these decisions? In the absence of an explanation, the woman has been left to navigate by herself through life. She is always trying to imagine, discover, ask, seek, to understand why. She is perpetually on a quest.

After years of engaging with women and listening to their life stories and questions, I began to recognize this common thread woven through their experiences and journeys, a thread that mirrored my own journey. I then turned to God for answers, not just for me, but for every woman.

Her Quest is the revelation that God gradually unveiled to me. The Lord revealed that this journey every woman is on started from the beginning of time and it is all recorded in the Bible. The Lord has not abandoned the woman, regardless of the silence and exclusion that surrounds her origin.

Naomi, Founder of Her Quest

Meet the Founder

Naomi Makumi.

Naomi Makumi is a woman of deep faith, vision, and purpose. As the founder of Her Quest, she carries a God-given mandate to help women recover their true identity — not the one shaped by circumstance, but the one written by God before time began.

Through years of ministry, personal journey, and anchored study of the Word, Naomi has crafted a framework that meets women exactly where they are — and walks them, step by step, into who they were always meant to be.

Beyond Her Quest, Naomi is an interior designer, a mentor to women, a conference speaker and a marriage counsellor — bringing the same care for beauty, order and wholeness to every space she serves in.
